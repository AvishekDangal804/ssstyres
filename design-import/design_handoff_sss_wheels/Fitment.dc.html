Part 3 — Find your own fitment

Read it straight off your vehicle

Your current setup already carries every number you need. Here's where to find each one before you order.

1
Check the tyre placard

Open the driver's door and look at the placard on the door jamb (or in the owner's manual). It lists the factory rim size, tyre size and recommended pressures — your safe starting point.

2
Count studs & measure PCD

Count the wheel studs. For an even count (6), measure centre-to-centre across two opposite studs. For 5-stud, measure from the back of one hole to the centre of the second one along. That's your bolt pattern, e.g. 6x139.7.

3
Read the back of the wheel

Most wheels are stamped on the inner barrel or spoke with the size and offset — look for markings like 17x8 and ET0 (or +0). Note them down along with your bolt pattern and you have a full spec.

Golden ruleBolt pattern and centre bore must match exactly. Size and offset can change within legal limits — but if you're lifting, going wider or unsure of the law in your state, confirm with us first.
