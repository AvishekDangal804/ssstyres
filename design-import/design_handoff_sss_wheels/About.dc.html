About us

Ten years of keeping the trade rolling

SSS Wheels & Tyres started as a two-person operation supplying tyres to local workshops. Today we supply wheels and tyres wholesale to panel shops, workshops, fleets and resellers across Australia — with our own wheel range built for the way Australians actually drive.

Built on straight answers and fast dispatch

We're not a marketplace and we're not a middleman. We hold our own stock in our Dandenong South warehouse, answer the phone with someone who knows fitment, and dispatch same-day on orders placed before 2pm.

That's why 2,500 of our 3,500+ customers keep coming back — and why our Google rating sits at 5.0.
